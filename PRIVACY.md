# Privacy Policy

Clipster contains no ads, analytics or trackers. It only talks to the Clipster server you configured: the public server at [clipster.cc](https://clipster.cc) or your own.

## Data sent to the server

- Your username and a hash of your password (used to log in, your password itself is never sent)
- Your clips, encrypted on your device. The server can't read them.
- For each clip: its format (text or image), the name of your device and the time it was shared

The server keeps only your newest clips (5 on the public server) and deletes older ones.

## Data on your device

Your username, server address and the hashes derived from your password are stored in the app's private storage. They are not included in backups. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them.

## Permissions

- **Internet:** to reach the Clipster server
- **Nearby devices** (Android 17+, only if your server is on your local network): to reach it
- **Storage** (Android 9 and older, only when you save an image): to save it to the gallery
