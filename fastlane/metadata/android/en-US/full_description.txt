Clipster is a multi platform cloud clipboard: copy a text or image on your smartphone and paste it on your desktop, or vice versa.

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li>Share texts and images from any app with the share menu, or share your clipboard</li>
<li>Get the last clip with one tap or from the app shortcut, or pick one from your recent clips</li>
<li>Clips are encrypted on your device, the server only stores encrypted data</li>
<li>Use the public server at clipster.cc or run your own Clipster-Server, also with a self-signed certificate</li>
<li>No ads, no analytics, no trackers</li>
</ul>

Clients are available for Android, Linux, macOS, Windows and all browsers. Source code and the desktop client: https://github.com/mc51
